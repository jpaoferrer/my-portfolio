The Situation
From Spreadsheet Chaos to a Single Operations Brain
Aussie Mortgage Group runs 5 specialist brokers across NSW, VIC, QLD & WA, previously managed by spreadsheets, email threads, phone calls and shared folders.
🐌
Slow & inconsistent
Broker onboarding took weeks. Leads were assigned by gut feel, and some were simply forgotten before anyone followed up.
🕳️
Zero visibility
Nobody could answer "where is every loan right now?" Compliance records were scattered and audit-readiness was a fire drill.
📚
Buried in admin
Management had no reporting and the team spent its capacity coordinating instead of closing loans.

Module Breakdown
7 Interconnected Workflows
Five business modules plus two cross-cutting layers, every one writing to the same central database.
M1 · Webhook
🧑‍💼
Broker Onboarding
A broker application auto-creates their record, a Google Drive folder, an onboarding checklist, a welcome email and an ops alert, then chases missing items until they're compliant.
AirtableDriveGmailDiscord
M2 · Webhook · AI Flagship
🎯
Lead Intake & AI Assignment
The centrepiece. Every lead is captured, then AI categorises it (First Home Buyer / Refinance / Investment / Self-Employed / Commercial), scores it 0-100, and a matching engine assigns the best broker by specialty + state + live workload. The broker is notified instantly by email & Discord and a calendar follow-up is created, so no lead is ever forgotten.
OpenAILead ScoringSmart RoutingCalendarDiscord
M3 · Webhook + Cron
📈
Loan Pipeline Management
Tracks 8 stages from New Lead to Settlement. Timestamps every move, measures time-in-stage, and a daily sweep nudges stalled files, escalating real bottlenecks to the ops manager.
AirtableSLA TrackingAuto-Escalation

🛡️
AI Compliance Monitoring
When a broker marks a file ready, AI checks the 6 mandatory artefacts (incl. Best Interest Duty), detects missing documents, writes an audit-ready summary and flags risk, before a human officer signs off.

Business Impact
The Benefits
What the aggregator gains the day this goes live.
⏱️
15+ hrs/week saved
No more manual record creation, lead routing, follow-up chasing, file-checking or report building. The system does the admin; the team sells loans. Roughly a 60-70% cut in operational admin.
⚡
No lead left behind
Every lead is scored and assigned in under 60 seconds, with an enforced follow-up reminder. Warm enquiries stop going cold, directly protecting revenue.
🛡️
Always audit-ready
AI pre-screens every file for the 6 mandatory compliance artefacts and logs an explainable record. Compliance becomes a continuous state, not an annual fire drill.
👁️
Total visibility
The owner sees broker performance, the lead funnel, pipeline health and compliance risk in real time, and gets a weekly summary in their inbox without lifting a finger.
⚖️
Fair, consistent routing
Leads are matched on specialty, state and live workload, balancing distribution across brokers and removing the bias and guesswork of manual assignment.
💸
Enterprise-grade for ~$20/mo
Built entirely on free and low-cost tools. A complete digital operations manager for the price of a couple of coffees a week, and it scales without a rebuild.

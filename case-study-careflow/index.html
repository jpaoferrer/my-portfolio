Workflow Breakdown
8 Automated Pipelines
Each pipeline runs automatically, triggered by an event or a schedule, and shares one source of truth: a Google Sheets database.
P1 · Form Trigger
📋
Client Intake & Onboarding
A new referral submits an inquiry form. AI classifies urgency and care level, an intake packet is emailed to the family automatically, and the coordinator is notified, within minutes, with zero manual data entry.
Google FormsGroq AIGmail
P2 · Form Trigger
🧑‍⚕️
Caregiver Onboarding & Documents
New applications are screened by AI (qualified / needs review / not qualified), a personalized document checklist is emailed automatically, and a welcome message fires the moment onboarding is complete.
Google FormsGroq AIAuto-Triage
P3 · Smart Matching
🧠
AI Smart Scheduling Engine
AI reviews every active caregiver against a client's required skills, zone, availability, and existing schedule, then recommends the best match with a stated reason and creates the calendar event automatically.
Groq AIGoogle CalendarSmart Matching
P4 · Telegram Trigger
⏱️
Clock-In / Clock-Out + EVV
Caregivers clock in and out with one command from their phone. The system timestamps the visit, calculates hours, alerts the coordinator when someone is 15+ minutes late, and produces the EVV compliance record.
Telegram BotEVV ComplianceReal-Time
P5 · AI Structuring
📝
AI Care Notes Processor
Caregivers submit visit notes in plain language. AI restructures them into clinical SOAP format, checks them against the client's care plan for completeness, and flags the coordinator if anything concerning appears.
Groq AISOAP NotesAuto-Flagging
P6 · Event-Driven
💬
Family Communication Hub
The moment a caregiver clocks out, the family automatically receives a visit summary. Every Sunday, each family also receives a consolidated weekly summary, no one has to remember to send an update.
GmailGroq AIAuto-Routing
P7 · Daily · 7 AM
🛡️
Compliance & Certification Tracker
Every morning, the system checks every caregiver's HHA, CPR, TB, background check, and HIPAA status against expiry dates, sending 60/30/14-day renewal reminders and escalating missed deadlines automatically.
Groq AIComplianceAuto-Alerts
P8 · Weekly · Monday 8 AM
📊
Weekly Operations Intelligence Report
The centerpiece. Every Monday, the system pulls a full week of data, hours vs. scheduled, missed visits, late clock-ins, outstanding notes, expiring certifications, and AI writes a plain-English executive summary with action items, delivered straight to the owner's inbox. No dashboards, no manual compiling, just answers.
